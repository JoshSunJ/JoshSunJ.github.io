[ 02 / ABOUT ]THE PERSON BEHIND THE COMMITS

About Joshua Sun

I study Applied Mathematics at San José State University with a minor in Computer Science. AI is the area I’m most excited about: the mathematics behind frontier models and the engineering that turns their capabilities into useful tools.

THE THEORY

I’m interested in representation learning, generalization, and the limits of model expressivity. I’m especially curious about how reinforcement learning and additional computation at inference time shape a model’s reasoning, and how we can test whether it generalizes to unfamiliar problems.

THE APPLICATIONS

I’m drawn to agents that use tools, retrieval and context management, and multimodal systems that connect language, vision, and hardware. I want to build AI that can plan and act, then evaluate it with clear, repeatable tests. Exploring those ideas through papers, small experiments, and working software is the direction I want to take.
